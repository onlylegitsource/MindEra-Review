01Why we exist

University should be more than a classroom.

For most African students, getting ahead means stitching together systems that were never designed to work together. Coursework lives in one place. Skills are learned somewhere else, if at all. Opportunities depend on who you happen to know.

The students who make it through are often the ones who figured out the system on their own. That should not be the requirement.

  1. 01Academic resourcesscattered across group chats and shared files
  2. 02Practical skillsself-taught, if you know where to look
  3. 03Campus informationpassed on by word of mouth
  4. 04Opportunitiesfound through connections most students lack
  5. 05Gigs and paid workrarely designed with students in mind
  6. 06Professional developmentpostponed until after graduation

Nelavista is being built to bring these experiences together, around how students actually move through university.

02Nelavista

Nelavista is a platform built specifically for university students. Learn your courses, build real-world skills, find opportunities and navigate campus, all in one place.

  2. 02

    Skills

  3. 03

    Opportunities

    Connecting students to internships, projects, competitions, work opportunities, and other paths beyond the classroom.

  4. 04

    Campus

    Helping students navigate university life and get to the campus information that matters, when it matters.

Where we are

645+

Nelavista is early, and that is deliberate. We are building with students, learning from how they use the platform, and expanding one pillar at a time.

The direction is clear: from a student platform toward shared infrastructure for the whole journey, from first lecture to first paycheck. We use AI where it genuinely helps students learn and find their way, but the product is about students, not the technology.

03The economic opportunity

Students should not have to wait until graduation to become economically valuable.

Universities hold some of the largest concentrations of capable, connected young talent on the continent. Most of that capacity sits idle until a degree is finished, and often long after.

Nelavista wants to help universities become channels through which students access real work: structured, paid, and matched to what they can actually do.

  1. Learn

    Academia + Skills

    Understand the coursework. Build the practical skills employers and clients pay for.

  2. Prove

    Evidence of ability

    Turn what a student can do into something others can see and trust.

  3. Earn

    Opportunities + Work

    Connect that ability to projects, gigs and remote work, while still in school.

05Founder

Built from the student side of the problem.

Abdul Afeez Tella is a Nigerian university student, entrepreneur and builder. He is building Nelavista from inside the system it is designed to fix.

Why he started

As a student, he saw how much of university success depends on information and access that are unevenly shared. Capable students fall behind not for lack of ability, but for lack of a clear path.

What is broken

Learning, skills and opportunity live in separate places, and the bridge from campus to real work is left to luck and personal connections. Talent is everywhere. Structured access is not.

What he is building

Nelavista: one platform where students can learn their courses, build practical skills, navigate campus and find opportunities, with a growing focus on real, paid work.

Where it is going

Toward infrastructure for the university-to-work transition across Africa, connecting student talent to the knowledge, skills, opportunities and networks it needs.

The people best placed to close the gap between university and work are the ones standing in it.

06Vision

The university-to-work transition should be an infrastructure problem, not a luck problem.

Nelavista aims to become a major platform connecting African students to the knowledge, skills, opportunities and networks they need to move from university into meaningful work. Not as a lucky break for a few, but as a dependable path for many.
